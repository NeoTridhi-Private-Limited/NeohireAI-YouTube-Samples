# Crack the HR Interview: Strengths, Weaknesses, and Why Should We Hire You? (practice sheet)

A NeoTridhi course on the HR round and other formal interviews. This folder holds the practice material for this video.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| answer_sheet.md | Every question from the video, the structure of a strong answer, one example answer, and blank lines for your own answer |

How to use it:
1. Watch the video once.
2. Before writing "Why should we hire you?" and "Why do you want to work for us?", research the company:
   its website and recent news, the job post (pick their top two needs), and reviews from employees.
3. Write your own answer under each question, using your real work and real proof.
4. Prepare two weaknesses, not one. Say every answer aloud, and ask a friend to push you with one follow-up question.

The example answers are templates: make them your own and keep them true. Never invent experience.

General interview guidance, not a guarantee of any outcome. Practices and laws differ by country, company and role.
