# Stress, Pressure and Curveballs: answer sheet

From the NeoTridhi course "Crack the HR Interview" (the video on stress and pressure in interviews).

**What's here**
- `answer_sheet.md`: every interview question from the video (handling stress, tight deadlines, a missed deadline, a cold
  interviewer who interrupts, sell me this pen, a guesstimate, everything urgent at once, why should we not hire you), the
  structure of a strong answer, one example answer each, and blank lines for your own answer. Also the five steps for when your
  mind goes blank, and a table of six more pressure questions with quick tips.

**How to use it**
1. Print it or copy it into your notes.
2. For each question, write your method and one true example from work, college or home.
3. Say each answer aloud. Aim for about one minute, then stop.
4. Practise with a friend or family member who plays a cold interviewer: they interrupt, look bored and say "that sounds
   prepared". Practise answering shorter, not louder.
5. For curveballs, practise thinking aloud: say each assumption before you do the maths.

**Honesty**
The example candidates are made up to show the structure, and the guesstimate numbers are assumptions, not facts. Use only true
stories of your own. Advice here is general and does not guarantee any outcome.
