# Formal and Panel Interviews: answer sheet

From the NeoTridhi course "Crack the HR Interview" (the video on formal and panel interviews).

**What's here**
- `answer_sheet.md`: every interview question from the video (why an officer, your hometown, a question you can't answer,
  current affairs, why an MBA, a hospital panel, a group discussion, introducing yourself formally), the structure of a strong
  answer, one example answer, and blank lines for your own answer. Also tips for facing a panel and a table of six more formal
  questions.

**How to use it**
1. Print it or copy it into your notes.
2. Prepare your background: your district or hometown, the basics of your field, and two or three news topics from both sides.
3. Write each answer in complete sentences, then say it aloud in a calm, formal voice. Aim for about one minute.
4. Practise with two or three friends acting as a panel; look at the one who asked, include the others, finish with the one who asked.
5. For group discussions, practise entering in a pause, building on someone by name, and giving a fair summary.

**Honesty**
The example candidates are made up to show the structure. Use only true stories and facts you have checked. If you don't know an
answer, say so calmly; never bluff. Advice here is general and does not guarantee any outcome.
