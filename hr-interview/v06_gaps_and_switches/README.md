# Gaps and Switches: answer sheet

From the NeoTridhi course "Crack the HR Interview" (the video on career gaps and job switches).

**What's here**
- `answer_sheet.md`: every interview question from the video (gaps, returning after a break, frequent job changes,
  "Why are you leaving?", layoffs, relocation, shifts, notice period, interviewing elsewhere), the structure of a strong
  answer, one example answer, and blank lines for your own answer. Also truthful one-liners for common gaps and six more questions.

**How to use it**
1. Write your gap line first: one truthful sentence for each gap on your resume.
2. Check your resume dates against your offer letters and relieving letters. They must match.
3. Read your employment contract for the notice period and any buyout or early-release rules before you answer.
4. Say each answer aloud until it sounds calm. Calm tells the interviewer it's really behind you.

**Honesty**
The example candidates are made up to show the structure. Never invent a job, an offer or a business to fill a gap.
Notice periods, buyouts and employment rules differ by company and by country: check yours. Advice here is general and does
not guarantee any outcome.
