# Crack the HR Interview: Gaps and Switches (answer sheet)

Write your lines before the interview and say them aloud until they feel calm. Everything here must be true:
employment dates are often checked, and a story that changes is worse than any gap.

**The pattern for explaining any gap (four steps)**
1. The reason, in one truthful line (you don't owe anyone private medical or family details).
2. What you did to stay current.
3. Why you are ready now.
4. Back to this role and this company.

Never bad-mouth a past employer. Promise only dates, moves and shifts you can keep.

---

## 1. I see a gap in your resume. What happened?

**Strong answer structure:** one truthful line on the reason -> what you did to stay current -> ready now -> link to the role.

**Example (Karthik, a software tester in Chennai, a two-year gap):**
"My father had a serious illness and I was his main carer. He has recovered now. During that time I kept my testing skills
current: I completed a test automation course and practised on open source projects in the evenings. I'm ready to work full time
now, and this role uses the automation I've been learning."

**Truthful one-liners for common gaps**

| Reason | One honest line |
|---|---|
| Illness | "I was unwell. I've recovered, and I'm ready." |
| Family care | "I cared for a family member full time." |
| Exam preparation | "I prepared seriously for an exam, and here's what I learned." |
| Layoff | "My role was cut in a restructuring." |
| A planned break | "I took a planned break, and during it I did this." |
| A startup that closed | "I built something, it closed, and here's what I learned." |

## 2. Tell me about the years since your last job. (returning after a break)

**Strong answer structure:** a choice, not an apology -> what you refreshed recently -> the role you want now and why this team.

**Example:**

## 5. I see you were laid off. Tell me about that.

**Strong answer structure:** say it plainly (a business decision) -> what you achieved before it -> what you've done since. If it was about performance, don't call it a layoff: say what you've improved.

**Example (Jessica, a recruiter in Chicago):**
"The company reduced its hiring team, and my role was one of those cut. Before that, I filled my roles within the target times
and trained two new recruiters. Since then, I've completed a certification and done some contract recruiting."

## 8. What is your notice period?

**Strong answer structure:** the real notice period -> you'll ask for an early release -> you'll check your contract and any buyout policy -> you'll confirm quickly. If you're already serving notice, give the real last day.

**Example:**
"My notice period is ninety days. I'll ask my company for an early release. Some companies also allow a notice-period buyout,
so I'll check my contract and update you quickly."
Rules and contracts differ by company and by country: check yours.

## 9. Are you interviewing elsewhere?

**Strong answer structure:** the truth, calmly -> this role is your first choice (if it is) -> a real reason. Never invent an offer to create pressure. If another offer arrives, say so honestly and ask about their timeline.

**Example:**
"Yes, I'm in early stages with a couple of companies. This role is my first choice, because of the automation work your team is doing."

## More gap and switch questions (quick tips)

| Question | Quick tip | Your answer (notes) |
|---|---|---|
| Why did you leave after only six months? | One honest reason, one lesson. | |
| What did you do during the gap? | Be specific: learning, caring, building. | |
| Why switch from teaching to tech (or any career change)? | The thread that connects both. | |
| Can you join earlier? | Only promise what you can really do. | |
| Would you take a lower title? | Focus on the work and the growth. | |
| Why weren't you promoted? | Facts, no blame, and what you're doing about it. | |

## Four things to remember
- A gap is a fact: explain it in one truthful line.
- Show what you did, and why you're ready now.
- Never bad-mouth a past employer.
- Promise only the dates and moves you can keep.
