# Video and AI interviews: practice sample

Crack the HR Interview (NeoTridhi YouTube course), the video on video calls and AI-led interviews.

## What is here

- `answer_sheet.md`: every question from the video, the structure of a strong answer, one example answer,
  and blank "Your answer:" lines. Also the setup checklist, questions to ask at the end, and email templates.

## How to use it

1. Do the setup checklist on the laptop you will really use.
2. Write your own answer to each question. Keep it truthful and in your own words.
3. Say each answer out loud with a timer (60 to 90 seconds for story questions).
4. Record one practice answer on your laptop and watch it once. Look for filler words, eye contact and light.
5. Pick your two end-of-interview questions and draft your thank-you email before the day.

AI interview platforms (for example NeohireAI.com) work differently from each other: always read the instructions
each platform gives you. The examples are illustrations, not scripts to memorise, and no answer guarantees an offer.
