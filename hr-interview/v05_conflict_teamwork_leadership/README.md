# Conflict, Teamwork and Leadership: answer sheet

From the NeoTridhi course "Crack the HR Interview" (the video on conflict, teamwork and leadership).

**What's here**
- `answer_sheet.md`: every interview question from the video, the structure of a strong answer, one example answer, and blank
  lines for your own answer. Also a table of six more team questions with quick tips.

**How to use it**
1. Print it or copy it into your notes.
2. For each question, pick a real story from your work, college, internship or volunteering.
3. Write it with the five-step pattern: situation, why it mattered, what you did, the result, the lesson.
4. Say each answer aloud. Aim for about one minute, then stop and let the interviewer ask.
5. Practise with a friend who interrupts you with follow-up questions.

**Honesty**
The example candidates are made up to show the structure. Use only true stories of your own; interviewers ask follow-up
questions, and a story you didn't live falls apart quickly. Advice here is general and does not guarantee any outcome.
