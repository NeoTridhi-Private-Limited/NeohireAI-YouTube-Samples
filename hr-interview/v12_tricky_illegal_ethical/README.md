# Tricky, illegal and ethical questions: practice sample

Crack the HR Interview (NeoTridhi YouTube course), the video on personal and illegal questions, ethics,
background verification, offer letters, declining offers and resigning.

## What is here

- `answer_sheet.md`: every question from the video, the structure of a strong answer, one example answer,
  and blank "Your answer:" lines. Also the redirect pattern, a table of more tricky questions, the offer
  letter red flags, and acceptance, decline and resignation templates.

## How to use it

1. Read the redirect pattern first: stay calm, find the worry, answer the worry, back to the job.
2. Write your own answer to each question, in your own words. Every fact must be true.
3. Practise the personal questions out loud with a friend until you can answer calmly.
4. Before verification, check your resume dates and CTC against your relieving letters and payslips.
5. Before signing any offer, go through the red-flag list.

Rules about which questions employers may ask differ by country and state: check yours. This is not legal advice,
and no answer guarantees an offer.
