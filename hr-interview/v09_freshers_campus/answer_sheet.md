# Crack the HR Interview: Freshers and Campus HR (answer sheet)

Write your answers before placement day, not in the queue outside the HR cabin. Use real projects, internships, clubs and
part-time jobs. Keep each answer to about one minute. Never invent experience or deny a plan you really have: HR asks
follow-up questions, and offers can be withdrawn later.

**The pattern for every fresher answer**
1. Proof, not adjectives: one real example instead of "hardworking, quick learner, passionate".
2. Your own part in it ("I handled the database", not "we did everything").
3. What changed, or what you learned. No invented numbers: if you don't know the exact result, describe the change.
4. A link back to this job.

Be truthful about higher studies, bonds, the family business and where you can live. Read before you sign; ask before you agree.

## 1. You have no experience. Why should we hire you?

**Strong answer structure:** "I don't have work experience yet, but..." -> one real project, internship or responsibility -> your part in it -> what you learned -> why that helps in this role.

**Example (a fresher engineer in Pune):**
"I don't have work experience yet, but in my final year I built an attendance app with two friends. I handled the database and
fixed the bugs our testers found. We had a fixed submission date, so I learned to plan my work to a deadline. That's the kind of
work I'd do in this role, and I'd like to keep building on it."

## 5. This role has a service agreement (a bond). Are you comfortable with it?

**Strong answer structure:** positive about the commitment -> ask to read the agreement before signing -> ask about the period, the amount and the exit terms. Rules on these agreements differ by country and state: read it fully and ask someone you trust if unsure.

**Example:**
"I'm comfortable with a commitment, and I'd like to grow here. May I see the agreement before I sign? I'd like to understand the
period, the amount, and what happens if I need to leave early."

## More fresher questions (quick tips)

| Question | One-line tip |
|---|---|
| Why this branch of study? | The real reason, even if it was family advice, and what you came to enjoy |
| Do you have other offers? | Be truthful; you don't have to name the companies |
| What are your hobbies? | A real one: expect follow-up questions about it |
| Are you okay with shifts? | Ask what the shifts are, then answer honestly |
| Why should we pick you over your classmates? | Talk about what you bring; never put classmates down |
| What did you learn in college? | One skill, with one example |
