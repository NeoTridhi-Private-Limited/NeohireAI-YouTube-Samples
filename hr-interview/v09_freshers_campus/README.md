# Freshers and Campus HR: answer sheet

From the NeoTridhi course "Crack the HR Interview" (the video on freshers and campus HR interviews).

**What's here**
- `answer_sheet.md`: every interview question from the video (no experience, the internship, five years, higher studies,
  service agreements, the family business, relocation, a backlog), the structure of a strong answer, one example answer,
  and blank lines for your own answer. Also a table of six more fresher questions with quick tips.

**How to use it**
1. Print it or copy it into your notes before placement season.
2. For each question, pick something real: a project, an internship, a club, a part-time job, a responsibility at home.
3. Replace adjectives with proof: what you did yourself, what changed, what you learned.
4. Say each answer aloud. Aim for about one minute, then stop and let the interviewer ask.
5. Practise with a friend who asks one follow-up question after every answer.

**Honesty**
The example candidates are made up to show the structure. Use only true stories and true plans of your own. Be truthful about
higher studies, family plans and where you can live. Read any bond or service agreement fully before signing; the rules differ
by country and state. Advice here is general and does not guarantee any outcome.
