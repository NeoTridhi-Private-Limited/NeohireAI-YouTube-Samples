# Salary negotiation, part two: offers, counter-offers and walking away

The template sheet for this video of **Crack the HR Interview** (NeoTridhi, with Ava and Priya).

## What's here

- `answer_sheet.md`: every question from the video (9 offer-stage questions and 6 more in a table), the structure of a strong
  answer, one word-for-word example answer for each, and blank "Your answer" lines. Also: the five calm steps when an offer
  arrives, how to decline a counter-offer, what the offer letter must include, a short note on U S offers (base, bonus,
  equity), and a polite walk-away script.

## How to use it

1. Write down your floor before any offer call, and keep it to yourself.
2. Write your own answer under each question. Keep every fact true; never invent an offer.
3. Practise the scripts aloud, especially "Could you send the full offer in writing?" and stopping after your range.
4. When an offer arrives, check it against the letter checklist before you resign or sign.

## Honesty notes

- No salary figures are given anywhere, on purpose. Research current ranges for your city and role.
- Joining bonuses often have a payback clause; notice buyouts are supported by some employers and not others. Ask, and get it in writing.
- Rules on salary history, pay ranges and notice periods differ by country and place. Check yours.
- General guidance only, not a guarantee of any outcome.
