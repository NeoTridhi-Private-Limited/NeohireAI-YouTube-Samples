# Salary negotiation, part one: research, C T C and "What's your expected salary?"

The template sheet for this video of **Crack the HR Interview** (NeoTridhi, with Ava and Priya).

## What's here

- `answer_sheet.md`: every question from the video (8 mock-interview questions and 6 more in a table), the structure of a strong
  answer, one example answer for each, and blank "Your answer" lines. It also has a "three numbers" box (low, target, high) and a
  table to read your own C T C (fixed, variable, employer P F, gratuity, benefits, E sops, in hand).

## How to use it

1. Do your research first: salary sites (ranges people have reported, rough and sometimes old), job posts that show a range,
   friends and seniors in the same role and city (ask for a range, not their salary), and recruiters (ask for the band).
2. Fill in your three numbers and your own C T C table.
3. Write your own answer under each question. Keep every fact true.
4. Say each answer aloud, and practise stopping after you say your range.

## Honesty notes

- No salary figures are given anywhere, on purpose. Ranges change by city, role, company and year: research current ranges.
- Never lie about your current pay; background verification often checks it. Use truthful framing instead.
- Laws and practices differ by country (for example, some places restrict asking about salary history). Check yours.
- General guidance only, not a guarantee of any outcome.
