# Your Stories with the STAR Method: answer sheet

From the NeoTridhi course "Crack the HR Interview" (the video on challenges, failures, mistakes and proudest moments).

**What's here**
- `answer_sheet.md`: every interview question from the video (biggest challenge, a failure, proudest achievement, a mistake,
  what your manager would say, going beyond your job, learning something quickly), the STAR structure of a strong answer, one
  example answer each, and blank lines for your own answer. Also a table of six more "tell me about a time" questions with quick
  tips, and a blank story bank.

**How to use it**
1. Print it or copy it into your notes.
2. Fill the story bank with six to eight true stories from work, college, internships, volunteering or home.
3. Write each one in STAR form: Situation, Task, Action, Result (with a number if you have one) and the lesson.
4. Say each answer aloud. Aim for 60-90 seconds, then stop and let the interviewer ask.
5. Record yourself once on your phone and watch it. Cut anything before the situation that isn't needed.

**Honesty**
The example candidates are made up to show the structure. Use only true stories and real numbers of your own; interviewers ask
follow-up questions and some call references. Advice here is general and does not guarantee any outcome.
